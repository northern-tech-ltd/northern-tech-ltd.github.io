Services

We help businesses cut the manual work out of their operations. Engagements are scoped to the problem — we don't sell a fixed product. Common areas we work in are below.

Workflow & process automation

Removing repetitive steps from the way work actually gets done — client onboarding, invoicing and follow-ups, document handling, internal approvals, reporting. We use the automation platform that fits the job (n8n, Make, Zapier, or purpose-built code) rather than forcing everything through one tool.

Systems integration

Connecting the tools a business already relies on so data stops being re-typed between them — CRMs, accounting software, email, calendars, spreadsheets, storage, and custom line-of-business systems. Where an official integration doesn't exist, we build one.

Custom internal tools

When off-the-shelf software isn't quite right, we build lightweight internal tools, dashboards, and admin interfaces tailored to how a team actually works. We keep these small and focused rather than trying to replace an entire platform.

Practical AI

Applying large language models and other AI to tasks that used to need a person: extracting data from documents and emails, drafting replies, categorising inbound requests, summarising long threads, and building assistants that sit alongside existing workflows.

Ongoing support

Automations need looking after. We offer ongoing support and maintenance for the systems we build, and can adopt existing automations that a business has outgrown or inherited.
