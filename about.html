About

Northern Tech LTD is a private limited company registered in Scotland. We are an automation agency: we help businesses reduce the manual, repetitive work involved in running their operations.

What we do

Our engagements typically fall into three areas: automating existing workflows across the tools a business already uses, integrating systems that don't talk to each other, and building custom internal software where a bespoke tool is the right answer. Where useful, we apply large language models and other AI to tasks that were previously done by hand.

How we work

We're a small UK-based team. We start most engagements with a short discovery — mapping what actually happens today, where time is being lost, and what a realistic automation looks like — before proposing a scope of work. We prefer shipping small, useful improvements quickly over long build cycles.

Where we work

We work with clients across the UK and remotely with businesses further afield. Our infrastructure choices default to UK and EU hosting, and we build with UK data protection requirements in mind from the outset.
